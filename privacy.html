Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

English · 简体中文

Short version: Was Here does not collect any of your data. Your photos and everything you add in the app stay on your iPhone. We have no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Your photos

  • With your permission, Was Here reads the date and location of the photos and videos in your library to place them on your map. Hidden photos and Shared Albums are not read.
  • The app only reads your library. It never edits, moves or deletes a photo, in Photos or in iCloud Photos.
  • Your photos are never uploaded or copied anywhere. Thumbnails are shown directly from your Photos library.
  • The only time the app adds anything to your library is when you choose “Save Image” for a poster or trip guide you made. That adds one new image and touches nothing else.

Apple Maps

  • To show city names, the app asks Apple Maps for the place name of one photo location per area (roughly every 10 km). This request goes to Apple, not to us, and is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy. City names are then saved on your iPhone so they aren’t looked up again.
  • When you search for a place (for example, to place a photo that has no location), what you type is sent to Apple Maps to find matching places.
  • The map itself is provided by Apple Maps.

What you add in the app

Journal entries, moods, places and dates you set, cover photos, hidden photos, trip guide notes, your wishlist and goals are saved only in the app on your iPhone. They are included in your iPhone backup like other app data. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

If you turn them on, trip recap and journal reminders are created on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us.

Sharing

When you share a poster or trip guide, only that image or PDF is shared, with the app or person you choose.

Children

Was Here does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: see the Support page.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 24 日

简单来说：“到此一游”不收集你的任何数据。你的照片和你在 App 里添加的所有内容都只保存在你的 iPhone 上。我们没有服务器，没有账号，没有广告，也没有数据分析。

你的照片

  • 在你允许后，“到此一游”会读取图库中照片和视频的拍摄日期和地点，把它们放到你的地图上。不会读取已隐藏的照片和共享相簿。
  • App 只读取你的图库，绝不会修改、移动或删除任何照片，无论是在“照片”还是 iCloud 照片中。
  • 你的照片不会被上传或复制到任何地方。缩略图直接从你的照片图库显示。
  • 只有当你为自己制作的海报或旅行攻略选择“存储图像”时，App 才会在图库中新增一张图片，不会改动其他任何内容。

Apple 地图

  • 为了显示城市名称，App 会向 Apple 地图查询每个区域（大约每 10 公里）中一张照片所在位置的地名。这个请求发送给 Apple，而不是我们，受 Apple 隐私政策 约束。查到的城市名称会保存在你的 iPhone 上，不会重复查询。
  • 当你搜索地点时（例如为没有位置的照片设置地点），你输入的内容会发送给 Apple 地图来查找地点。
  • 地图本身由 Apple 地图提供。

你在 App 中添加的内容

日记、心情、你设置的地点和日期、封面照片、隐藏的照片、旅行攻略、心愿单和目标，都只保存在你 iPhone 上的 App 里。它们和其他 App 数据一样会包含在 iPhone 备份中。删除 App 会同时删除这些内容。

通知

如果你打开通知，旅程回顾和日记提醒都在你的 iPhone 上生成，不会向我们发送任何内容。

分享

当你分享海报或旅行攻略时，只会把这张图片或 PDF 分享给你选择的 App 或联系人。

儿童

“到此一游”不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

变更与联系

如果本政策有变更，新版本会发布在本页并更新日期。如有问题，请见 支持页面。
